Put everything in this folder on your website, exactly as it is (keep the folder structure).

  index.html, schedule/, prereqs/, clos/, data/   public pages and their data
  staff/                                          the password-protected staff area
     staff/*/data.enc.json                         encrypted: unreadable without the staff password

The staff area is not linked from the public pages. Send staff the address .../staff/ and the password.
Pages must be opened from a web address (https://...), not by double-clicking the files.
