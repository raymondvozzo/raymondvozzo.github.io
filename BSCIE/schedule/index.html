Dashed boxes are choices: click one to pick a course. ✓ marks a course as completed. ⚠ marks something to check (a prerequisite not taken earlier, a semester the course isn’t in, a repeat). ⇄ swaps a box with one in another semester. Colours and slot names are CurV’s. Semester availability comes from where CurV’s schedules place each course (known for 151 of 291 courses).
