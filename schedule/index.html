To open and edit this schedule online:

Dashed boxes are choices: click one to pick a course for it. Click the ✓ in a box’s corner to mark it as completed. A ⚠ marks something to check, such as a prerequisite that isn’t earlier in your schedule. Course data comes from the prerequisite tree. For the majors, Year 4 (Honours) opens when you click its heading. A ⇄ button on a box lets you swap it with a box in another semester. Year 4 boxes only swap within Year 4.
